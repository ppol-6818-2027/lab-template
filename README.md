# PPOL 6818 week 1: your pitch

Open `project.Rproj` in RStudio.

1. Write your slide in `pitch.md`, in your own words, at most 60 words: a title, the policy, where it varies, your question, and one line about you. Fill in your name and GitHub username at the top.
2. Optionally, ask the course agent (**Addins > Course agent**) to draw one figure from data with R code into `pitch.png`. The agent formats and draws; it does not write or edit your words.
3. In RStudio's **Git** pane, tick `pitch.md` (and `pitch.png`), click **Commit**, write a short message, then click **Push**. Push before 4:45pm on the day of class: at 4:45pm the course combines every slide into the class deck.
